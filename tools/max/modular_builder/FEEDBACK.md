# Feedback and test reports

Thanks for trying the project. Reports from real musical sessions are especially useful while the modular combinations are being explored.

Please include:

- builder version and generated firmware SHA-256;
- official stock input SHA-256 and selected feature groups or the adjacent selected-mod `.json`;
- Syntakt hardware/OS context and the exact steps that led to the result;
- expected and observed behavior;
- a photo of any exception screen, plus a short audio/video clip when it helps;
- whether the issue repeats after a cold boot and with a simple fresh project.

Please do not attach official Elektron firmware, generated `.syx` files, user projects, or personal material to public reports. A hash and the selected-mod JSON are enough to identify a build. Keep your backup and official firmware available while testing experimental configurations.
